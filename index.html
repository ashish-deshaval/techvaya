Visitor Management

  • Touchless QR/OTP check-in, pre-registration, face capture & instant badge printing
  • Automated host alerts, NDA/EHS safety sign-offs, watchlist checks & gatepass workflows
  • Real-time evacuation muster lists & centralized multi-site compliance dashboards

AI Video Intelligence

Video Analytics

AI-enabled video intelligence that turns camera streams into actionable events for security, workplace safety, operational awareness, and facility monitoring.

  • AI safety & security detection (PPE compliance, fire/smoke, intrusion & loitering)
  • High-accuracy ANPR/LPR vehicle access, facial recognition, people & queue counting
  • Seamless integration with existing & new cameras via unified VMS dashboards

Smart Buildings & Energy

EMS & BMS

Energy and building management capabilities for connected facilities, helping operations teams monitor assets, optimize power consumption, and unify facility control.

  • Unified monitoring & control of Smart HVAC, chillers, DG sets, transformers & utilities
  • Real-time energy sub-metering, load profiling, power quality & tariff optimization
  • IoT-to-cloud dashboards for predictive maintenance & sustainability reporting
OEM Platform
Real-Time Location

RTLS

Real-Time Location System capabilities for indoor and outdoor asset tracking, workforce safety, Forklift/MHE fleet visibility, and material movement across industrial environments.

  • Real-time indoor/outdoor tracking for assets, WIP inventory, Forklifts & MHE fleets
  • Workforce safety badges with SOS alerts, geo-fencing & automated emergency mustering
  • Multi-technology BLE, UWB, RFID & GPS architectures for complete plant visibility
OEM Platform
Physical Security

Perimeter Security

Integrated perimeter protection for industrial and enterprise sites, combining smart intrusion detection, integrated access control, and automated boundary protection.

  • Multi-layered boundary intrusion detection with monitored pulse fencing & vibration sensors
  • Integrated enterprise access control with automated camera verification & alarm workflows
  • Cyber-secure hardware controllers & centralized command-and-control visibility
